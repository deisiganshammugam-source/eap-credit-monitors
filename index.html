EAP credit monitors

Two interactive monitors built from public data (BIS, IMF, World Bank, central banks). Each opens on the regional view and drills down by economy. Draft for internal discussion.

  • Credit Cyclicality Monitor: does bank credit move with the business cycle? East Asia and Pacific against Asia-Pacific and emerging-market comparators, 21 economies.
  • Credit-to-GDP Gap Monitor: is credit running ahead of the economy? BIS credit-to-GDP gaps for 44 economies plus staff estimates with the same method.

The pages are self-contained (charts work offline once loaded) and are replaced after each data refresh.
